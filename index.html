About Me

— Who I am and what drives me —

I'm Alessandro Pucciariello, an Electronics Technician student in my final year at Instituto Madero, specializing in Telecommunications. I've always been fascinated by how electronic devices work — from the inside out.

I'm passionate about building solutions that bridge hardware and software. I love taking things apart to understand them, and even more, putting them back together better than before. My interests lie at the intersection of programming, electronics, and robotics.

One of my proudest achievements was building my own PC from scratch — I chose every component, assembled it myself, and even customized a gaming case with LEDs. I did all the wiring and cable management on my own.

I'm also deeply interested in computer repair, both hardware and software. I enjoy helping others fix their devices and learning about new technologies along the way.

Featured Project

— IoT System for Public Transport —

Bus Tracking System

GPS 4G IoT Passenger Counting Web Dashboard

An IoT device designed for public buses. It provides real-time GPS tracking to display the bus's real-time location and uses sensors to count passengers boarding and alighting, calculating real-time occupancy.

The system includes a 4G LTE module for communication, a cloud database for data storage, and a web dashboard for live monitoring.

🛠️ Technologies: GPS Module · 4G LTE Module · IoT Sensors · Cloud Database · Web Dashboard (HTML/CSS/JS) · Embedded C/Python
